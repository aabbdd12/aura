Statistics and econometrics you can read, run, and reproduce

AURA brings data management, estimation, survey statistics, poverty and inequality analysis, price-reform simulation and publication graphics into one free, command-driven environment — Python-native and reproducible.

What AURA does

From raw data to the published table and graph

Every command has a dialog box that writes it for you, and help with examples you can run. See all the commands →

Estimation

regress, logit, probit, poisson, panel (xtreg, hausman) and instrumental variables — robust and clustered errors, factor variables.

Tables of results

eststo and esttab put models side by side, to the screen or to LaTeX, HTML, Word and CSV.

Survey data

svyset designs and design-based standard errors for means, totals, ratios, proportions and regression.

Poverty and inequality

The DAAP package: FGT, Gini and Lorenz, polarization, pro-poor growth, dominance and decompositions.

Price reforms

The SUBSIM package: direct and indirect effects of price and subsidy reforms on well-being, poverty and revenue.

Graphics

Publication graphs edited with the mouse, merged into figures, copied to Word as vector drawings; interactive 3-D surfaces.

Data management

A workspace of several datasets: merge, reshape, collapse, by groups, labels and formats.

Scripts that re-run

.aur scripts with loops, macros and matrices, logs with their graphs, and every menu action written as a command.

Included packages

Two complete packages, in the box

DAAP — distributive analysis

Measure poverty and inequality, compare distributions, draw curves and decompose changes, with design-based standard errors throughout.

SUBSIM — price and subsidy reforms
